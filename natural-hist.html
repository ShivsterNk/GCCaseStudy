To Get To The Museum of Natural History

NOTE: While 81st Street is undergoing ADA-Station Improvements, those with ADA-Needs should instead take the bus.

Travel Note: For the American Museum of Natural History, Penn Station may be slightly faster than Grand Central.

Board a 168th Street bound Train, and get off at the 81st Street subway station.

The Subway and Bus Fare is $3.00. Pay with an OMNY Card or Contactless Payment, or a MetroCard.

MetroCards are also accepted, but can no longer be bought or refilled.


Choose which option to see how to get to Natural History.

By Subway

Exit from the platform your LIRR train arrives on and head to the mezzanine. Then, take the 47th Street escalators to the concourse.

Once there, find the exit for 47th Street, where you will end up on street level near 47th Street and Madison Avenue.

Do note, there are 2 staircases at this area, separated by the ticket machine.

Walk west along 47th Street, until you reach 6th Avenue.

Turn right and find the entrances to the 47th-50th Streets-Rockefeller Center subway station.

Get to the subway entrance, head for the turnstyles, pay and enter the subway!

OPTION 1: On weekdays, board an uptown Train, and get off at the 81st Street subway station.

OPTION 2: On weekends, board an uptown Train, and get off at the 59th Street-Columbus Circle subway station.

Then transfer to a 168th Street bound Train, and get off at the 81st Street subway station.

Get off the subway, exit the station, and head upstairs to the street level, finding the appropriate entrance for the museum.

By Bus

Exit from the platform your LIRR train arrives on to the mezzanine. Then, head up the 48th Street escalators to the concourse.

Then, take the elevator to street level, where you'll exit near the corner of 48th Street and Madison Avenue.

Walk northbound to 49th Street and Madison Avenue

There are 2 places you can board the bus:

Near East 49th Street and Madison - Northbound M3 or M4 Bus

Near East 50th Street and Madison - Northbound M1 or M2 Bus

Board any one of these buses:

Board an M1 Bus going to Harlem-147th Street
Board an M2 Bus going to Washington Heights
Board an M3 Bus going to Fort George
Board an M4 Bus going to the Cloisters
❮ ❯

Regardless of or which bus you take...

You MUST get off at East 79th Street and Madison Avenue!!

After getting off, walk back to 79th Street and board a westbound M79 Select Bus towards the West Side.

You can either tap with your OMNY or Contactless Payment to get on the M79 bus from your first tap.

Ride the M79 and get off at 81st Street and Central Park West.

You can walk to either entrance at the Museum


Some Tips and Tricks

At Grand Central, there is a set of elevators that will take you up from the mezzanine to the concourse.

It's located between the 46th Street and 47th Street escalator banks on the mezzanine, so look for signs.

There is another elevator available on the Concourse level, that will take you to the Street level.

This elevator is located in a small corner past the ticket at 47th Street, facing away from the 47th Street escalator bank.

Expected Time Taken - Minutes

To get onto street level from the LIRR tracks, it will take you roughly 5 Minutes depending on your speed and method you chose.

To get from the street level to Natural History by Subway, it will take you an additonal 19 Minutes, depending on the time of day.

To get from the street level to Natural History by Bus, it will take you an additonal 30 Minutes, depending on the time of day.
