Why Transit Matters To Me

Two main reasons.

1: To showcase my love for transit. Transit is one of my favorite modes of getting around.

While there are flaws within the transit system, it still is one of the most convenient ways to navigate your trips!

2: My dual intriusion of Grand Central. The famous terminal itself, and its new LIRR Sibling.

Why Does Grand Central Madison Matter?

To showcase the benefits of using the Grand Central Station to get around the city.

Common Misconceptions

Many commuters find this station gigantic, confusing, and difficult to navigate.

Many claim your brought to the middle of nowhere, and Grand Central barely offers benefits compared to Penn Station.

Many poke fun of Grand Central Madison being a mega-billion dollar bondoggle as its huge costs to low passenger count sticks out.

Many are enraged with the service patterns Grand Central caused, especially the banishment of through-Brooklyn service.

The Reality

While it may be huge and unfamiliar and a big change, I seek to help overcome the complex and its challenges!

By showing the benefits, getarounds, and tricks to navigating the complex, Grand Central will become much less scary!

I also want to show how to get to some attractions and places, that one would typically do from Penn Station.

The Benefits?

Some benefits to using Grand Central over Penn Station include:

Faster Access towards the East Side of Manhattan

Less Passenger Congestion and Crowding compared to Penn Station

Some places actually being faster to reach from Grand Central than Penn

Cleaner, newer, modern infrastructure
